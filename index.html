Automatic Mispronunciation Detector and Corrector

A machine-learning system that captures spoken audio, identifies mispronounced phonemes, and delivers precise corrective feedback to the speaker. Real-time speech input capture and audio preprocessing pipeline
Phoneme-level comparison using acoustic and linguistic models
Automated mispronunciation detection with confidence scoring
